Treatment Guide

Different areas require different techniques. Explore our recommendations for optimal results and minimal discomfort.

Hard Wax Threading

Delicate Facial Skin

Facial skin is extremely thin and prone to lifting. We exclusively use low-temperature hard wax that shrink-wraps the hair without adhering to the skin. For clients using retinoids, we highly recommend Threading.

  • Best for: Upper lip, chin, brows
  • Maintenance: Every 3-4 weeks
Soft Wax Sugaring

Arms & Legs

For large surface areas, strip wax (soft wax) is the most efficient and effective method. We use a botanical blend that spreads thinly and removes fine to coarse hairs swiftly.

  • Best for: Full legs, arms, back
  • Maintenance: Every 4-6 weeks
Hard Wax Sugaring

Intimate Areas

Comfort and speed are paramount. Our European hard wax is formulated specifically for coarse hair and sensitive skin, ensuring a clean removal with minimal discomfort.

  • Best for: Bikini, Brazilian, Underarms
  • Maintenance: Every 4 weeks
Sugaring

Highly Sensitive Skin

If you are prone to ingrown hairs, redness, or allergic reactions, organic Sugaring is the gold standard. The paste is all-natural, adheres only to the hair, and extracts in the direction of growth.

  • Best for: Reactive skin, entire body
  • Maintenance: Every 3-5 weeks
