Our Story

Redefining Hair Removal

Founded on the belief that body hair removal shouldn't be an agonizing chore. VelvetGlow was created to transform a clinical routine into a luxurious, restorative ritual.

We meticulously designed our sanctuary to alleviate the anxiety often associated with intimate waxing, utilizing European hard waxes and organic sugaring pastes that prioritize the integrity of your skin.

Our Philosophy

The four pillars that guide every treatment we perform.

Comfort

Heated beds, low-temperature wax, and swift techniques dramatically minimize discomfort.

Hygiene

Strict no-double-dipping policies. Medical-grade sterilization of all tools and treatment surfaces.

Confidence

A completely judgement-free zone where every body type is welcomed and celebrated.

Personalized Care

Every treatment begins with a skin analysis to determine the optimal removal method for you.

The 4-Step Approach

01

Analyze

We examine the hair growth pattern, length, and the condition of your skin barrier.

02

Cleanse & Prep

The area is thoroughly sanitized and a protective barrier oil is applied to shield the skin.

03

Execute

Working in small, swift sections to minimize discomfort while ensuring complete removal.

04

Soothe

A cooling, antibacterial serum is massaged into the open follicles to prevent inflammation.
