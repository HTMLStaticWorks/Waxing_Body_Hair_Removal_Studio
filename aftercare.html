Post-Treatment

Aftercare Guide

The secret to prolonged smoothness and preventing irritation lies in how you care for your skin after your session.

The Crucial First 24 Hours

Your hair follicles remain open and susceptible to bacteria and irritation immediately following treatment.

Avoid Excessive Heat

No hot baths, saunas, steam rooms, or tanning beds. Stick to lukewarm or cool showers to allow follicles to close properly.

Skip the Workout

Avoid intense workouts or activities that cause excessive sweating, as sweat contains bacteria that can easily enter open follicles.

Hands Off & Loose Fit

Avoid unnecessary touching of the treated area. Wear loose, breathable cotton clothing to prevent friction and allow the skin to breathe.

24–48 Hour Care Phase

Transitioning into maintenance to keep your skin hydrated and calm.

Gentle Cleansing

Wash the area with a mild, unscented cleanser. Avoid harsh soaps or shower gels with heavy fragrances.

Hydration & Calming

Apply aloe vera or a tea tree oil lotion to soothe any lingering redness. Keep the skin deeply moisturized with an organic body oil.

Wait to Exfoliate

Do NOT exfoliate the skin for at least 48 hours post-treatment. Your skin is already naturally exfoliated during the waxing process.

Long Term Care

Preventing Ingrowns

Consistent care is the key to preventing trapped hairs and bumps.

01

Exfoliate Regualrly

Starting 48-72 hours after treatment, use a gentle scrub or exfoliating mitt 2-3 times a week to remove dead skin cells that block hair growth.

02

Daily Moisture

Keep the skin soft and pliable. Dry, hardened skin forces growing hairs to curl back under the surface. Use a non-comedogenic lotion.

03

Avoid Friction

Tight clothing, especially right after treatment or during exercise, can push hairs back down into the follicles.

04

Treat Early

If you spot a bump, do not pick or squeeze. Apply a specialized ingrown serum containing salicylic or glycolic acid to gently release it.
