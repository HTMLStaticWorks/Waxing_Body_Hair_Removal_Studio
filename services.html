Specialty Techniques

Alternative ancient hair removal methods for sensitive skin and precise shaping.

Organic Sugaring

A 100% natural paste consisting only of sugar, lemon, and water. Molded against the hair growth and flicked off in the direction of growth. It only adheres to dead skin cells, making it incredibly gentle.

  • Prevents ingrown hairs
  • Safe for highly sensitive skin
  • Recommended for Brazilian & Underarms

Facial Threading

An ancient technique using antibacterial cotton thread to roll over lines of unwanted hair, plucking them at the follicle level. Unmatched for precision brow artistry.

  • Extreme precision shaping
  • Safe for clients using Retin-A / Accutane
  • Recommended for Brows & Full Face
